# One-Day Marathon Training Meal Plan

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete doing high-intensity endurance training with marathon preparation:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (130g), Fat (85g), Carbs (420g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (6:30 AM - Pre-Workout Fuel):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with honey (40g), banana (150g), and whole milk (240g). 2 eggs scrambled (100g).
    * Protein: 20g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **During Workout (8:00-10:30 AM - Hydration & Simple Carbs):** Sports drink (500ml) with 6% carbohydrate concentration (electrolyte drink), 1 energy gel (40g).
    * Protein: 0g, Fat: 0g, Carbs: 60g

* **Post-Workout Snack (10:45 AM - Recovery Window):** Chocolate milk (400g), granola bar (65g), and orange (150g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 95g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM - Carb Loading & Protein):** Pasta with marinara sauce (200g cooked pasta, 150g sauce), grilled chicken breast (140g), mixed green salad (100g) with olive oil dressing (15g), and white bread roll (50g).
    * Protein: 45g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 110g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM - Sustained Energy):** Rice cakes (60g) with almond butter (30g), apple (120g), and coconut water (250ml).
    * Protein: 10g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 70g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM - Recovery & Repair):** Baked salmon (150g), sweet potato (200g roasted), steamed broccoli (150g), and brown rice (80g cooked weight) with light butter (10g).
    * Protein: 35g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 75g

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM - Overnight Recovery):** Greek yogurt (200g, full-fat), granola (30g), and berries (80g).
    * Protein: 20g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 35g

**Hydration Strategy:** Drink 500ml water every hour throughout the day, with electrolyte beverage during/after the workout. Total fluid intake: 3-4 liters.

This meal plan strategically times carbohydrate intake around the high-intensity workout, includes adequate protein for muscle recovery, and maintains consistent energy levels throughout the marathon training day.
